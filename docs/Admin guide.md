# Admin guide

This page is for the people who run the site day to day. You do not need to know how to code. Sign in at `/login` with your admin email.

<p align="center"><a href="images/admin-dashboard.jpg"><img src="images/admin-dashboard.jpg" alt="The admin Today page" width="100%"></a></p>

## Today

The first page lists the people waiting for you: buyers who asked about a property, and owners who want to sell. Each one has **Reply on WhatsApp** and **Call** buttons, and **Done** when you have dealt with it.

## Add a listing

1. Press **+ Add a new listing**.
2. Fill in the basics: the name, what you are selling, the district, the town, the price and how the price is counted (per perch, total and so on).
3. Tap the photo box and choose photos from your phone or computer. They are made smaller on the way up. The first photo is the main one: good light, no people, show the road and the plot.
4. Choose **Yes, show it**, **Sold out** or **No, hide it**, and press **Save**.

It is on the website straight away. You can hide it or change it at any time.

### Extra details that sell

On the same page you can add:

* **Plots and prices**: one row for each numbered plot, with its size in perches, its price and whether it is available, reserved or sold.
* **Highlights**: the short reasons to buy, one per line.
* **What is nearby**: places and how far, for example `Galle town – 20 min`.
* **A map link** and **a video link**. Only Google Maps links, and YouTube, Facebook or TikTok links, are accepted. A video loads only when a visitor taps it.
* **A brochure** (PDF) and the **payment plan**: the down payment percentage and the number of months.

Every listing also has a printable **flyer** and a link to share on WhatsApp.

## Add from WhatsApp

<p align="center"><a href="images/admin-import.jpg"><img src="images/admin-import.jpg" alt="Add listings from WhatsApp" width="100%"></a></p>

* **A whole chat:** export the WhatsApp chat where you post your listings (with media), choose the `.zip` file, check the list of listings it found, and press **Create drafts**. Every listing message becomes a hidden draft with its photos.
* **One message:** paste it in the box and press **Create draft listing**. You get a hidden draft with the district, size, price and papers filled in.

Check each draft, fix what is wrong, and publish it. Nothing goes live until you say so.

## Banners and ads

Under **Banners & ads** you can show a message on the home page hero, a banner under it, or a sponsored slot between listings. Give each one a headline, a link, an optional picture or video, and start and end dates. Leave the dates empty to run it always. **Pause** hides it without deleting it.

## Enquiries and sellers

* **Inquiries** are the questions from listing pages. Reply on WhatsApp, call, or email, and set the status to new, contacted or closed. **Download as Excel (CSV)** saves them all.
* **Sell-your-land leads** are owners who filled in the form, with their photos. Mark them contacted or closed the same way.

## After-sales: customers and purchases

<p align="center"><a href="images/admin-customer.jpg"><img src="images/admin-customer.jpg" alt="One customer with payments and documents" width="100%"></a></p>

1. Under **Customers & purchases**, add a customer account: a name, a phone number, an email (it is their login) and a temporary password of at least 8 characters.
2. Open the customer, choose the project, type the plot and the total price, and add the purchase.
3. As money arrives, **Record payment** with the date, a description, the amount and, if you have it, the receipt (a PDF or a picture).
4. Upload documents such as the sale agreement and the deed. The customer sees them in their portal.
5. Set the **handover stage** (Booking confirmed, Agreement signed, Payments in progress, Deed preparation, Handover) and the **next instalment due** date.
6. If a customer forgets their password, set a new temporary one.

## Support tickets

Customers can open a ticket from their portal. Under **Support tickets** you read it, reply, and close it when it is solved.

## Contact and advisor

Under **Contact & advisor** you set the WhatsApp number every button uses, the phone number shown to visitors, the advisor's name, title, reply hours and photo, the office address and your social pages. The change shows on every page at once.

## Your password

Under **My password** you can change it. Use a long one that you do not use anywhere else.
